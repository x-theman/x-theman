How it works

01

Connect

Connect your Ethereum wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, etc.). The site only reads your address. It never asks for approvals or signatures other than the claim itself.

02

Check

Your address is checked against the published allocation list. Each entry includes a Merkle proof verified on-chain.

03

Claim

Send one transaction to the distributor contract. Tokens go straight to the eligible address, and you only pay gas.

FAQ

Who is eligible?

Allocations went to addresses that met the snapshot criteria: protocol users, contributors and community members. The full list is published in data/claims.json.

Is there a deadline?

Yes. Unclaimed tokens return to the treasury after the claim window closes. The countdown above shows the time remaining.

Do I need to pay anything?

Only network gas for the claim transaction. No one from the team will ever ask you to send tokens or share your seed phrase.

Can I claim to a different address?

No. Tokens are always sent to the eligible address encoded in the Merkle leaf, whoever submits the transaction.

How do I stay safe?

Always check the URL. This site never requests token approvals, setApprovalForAll or permit signatures. If a site asks for those while claiming an airdrop, close it.
